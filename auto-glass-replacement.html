Safety First

The Importance of Windshield Replacement

Your windshield is an integral part of your vehicle's safety. It shields you from the elements and gives you a clear view of the road ahead. A cracked windshield impairs your eyesight — and if the damage is severe, the glass can fail entirely.

As a rule: cracks smaller than one inch in diameter can often be repaired. Larger cracks, or damage that begins spreading across the windshield, mean it's time to replace. Schedule a mobile appointment as soon as you notice damage.
